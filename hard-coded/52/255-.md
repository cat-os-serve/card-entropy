# 52-deck, 0-255 bits of entropy extracted
A 52-deck is a deck of 52 cards, with the four suits, each of 13 cards, all unique. A fully shuffled 52-deck can supply around 225.6 bits of entropy.

## 32 bits of entropy required
Take a subset of any 16 cards from the 52-deck, shuffle those 16, and read out the output. This will produce around 44.25 bits of entropy.

## 64 bits of entropy required
Take a subset of any 23 cards from the 52-deck, shuffle those 23, and read out the output. This will produce around 74.45 bits of entropy.

## 96 bits of entropy required
Take a subset of any 30 cards from the 52-deck, shuffle those 30, and read out the output. This will produce around 107.71 bits of entropy.

## 128 bits of entropy required
Take a subset of any 36 cards from the 52-deck, shuffle those 36, and read out the output. This will produce around 138.09 bits of entropy.

## 160 bits of entropy required
Take a subset of any 43 cards from the 52-deck, shuffle those 43, and read out the output. This will produce around
175.33 bits of entropy.

## 192 bits of entropy required
Take a subset of any 48 cards from the 52-deck, shuffle those 48, and read out the output. This will produce around
202.95 bits of entropy.

## 224 bits of entropy required
Take the entire 52-deck, shuffle it, and read out the output. Then take a subset of any 6 cards from the 52-deck,
shuffle those 6, and read out the output. This will produce around 235.07 bits of entropy.
