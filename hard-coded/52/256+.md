# 52-deck, 256-1024 bits of entropy extracted
A 52-deck is a deck of 52 cards, with the four suits, each of 13 cards, all unique. A fully shuffled 52-deck can supply around 225.6 bits of entropy.

## 256 bits of entropy required
Take the entire 52-deck, shuffle it, and read out the output. Then take a subset of any 16 cards from the 52-deck, shuffle it, and read out the output. This will produce around 269.83 bits of entropy.
